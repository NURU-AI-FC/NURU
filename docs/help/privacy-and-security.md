# Your privacy and security

## Signing in

1. Enter your email and password.
2. NURU emails you a 6 digit code. Enter it to finish signing in.

The code works once and runs out after 10 minutes. After 5 wrong tries, ask for a new one.

## What is encrypted

- **Chats.** Your chats with NURU are encrypted with a key unique to you. Only you can read them. Zenith staff cannot see them.
- **Face check.** NURU keeps a face template, not a photo, and it is encrypted.
- **PIN.** Your transaction PIN is stored hashed, never as the digits.

## What NURU can see

- The balances and transactions from accounts you link, and only what you allowed.
- A summary NURU works out from them. Account numbers are masked before the assistant sees it.
- For BVN or NIN checks, only the last 4 digits are stored.

## What NURU cannot see or do

- It never sees or stores your bank password.
- It cannot move money from a linked account.
- It never moves money from a chat message alone. Every transfer needs your PIN and a face check.
- Your chats are not used to train AI models, and NURU never sends your data to advertisers.

## Your controls

In **Settings**, then **Privacy and data**, you can:

1. Turn **Save chat history** off. This deletes saved chats.
2. **Download my data** as a file.
3. **Delete chat history**.

If something looks wrong, tap **Freeze my account** in **Settings**, then **Security**. Transfers stop for 24 hours and your other devices are signed out.
