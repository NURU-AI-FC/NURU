# Sending money from Ask NURU

You can ask NURU to send money in a chat. NURU never moves money from a chat message alone. Every transfer needs your transaction PIN and a face check.

## Send money

1. Open **Ask NURU** and type something like "Send ₦5,000 to Tunde".
2. NURU shows a transfer card. A saved payee is filled in. For someone new, enter the **Bank**, the 10 digit **Account number** and the **Account name**.
3. Fill in **What is it for?** and check the amount and the **From** account.
4. Tap **Confirm and send**, or the review button for someone new.
5. Look at the camera for the face check, then enter your 4 digit PIN.

NURU then replies with the amount and a reference number.

## Your first transfer

The first time, NURU sets up both checks with you:

1. Choose a 4 digit PIN and enter it again. Avoid 1234 or repeated digits.
2. Allow the camera and hold still while NURU captures your face.

## If a check fails

- A wrong PIN or face stops the transfer. Nothing is sent, and NURU shows the tries you have left.
- After 3 failed checks, transfers pause for 30 minutes. Try again after that.

## If NURU pauses a transfer

A large payment to someone you have never paid can look like a scam. NURU pauses it and your money stays put. Tap **Cancel transfer**, or confirm with your face and PIN again if you know the person.

Zenith staff will never ask you to move money to a "safe account".
