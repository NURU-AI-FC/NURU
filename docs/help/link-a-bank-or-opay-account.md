# Linking a bank or OPay account

Linking an account lets NURU read your balances and transactions to build your insights. The link is read only. NURU can never move money from a linked account.

You can link Zenith Bank, GTBank, Access Bank, First Bank, UBA, Kuda, OPay or Moniepoint.

## Link an account

1. Open **Settings**, then **Linked accounts**. On the phone app, open **Settings**, then **Connected accounts**.
2. Tap **Link another account**.
3. Pick your bank or wallet, for example **OPay**.
4. Enter your internet banking **User ID** and **Password**, then tap **Log in securely**. Your login goes straight to the bank through Mono. NURU never sees or stores your bank password.
5. On **Allow NURU to read**, choose what NURU can see. **Balances** and **Transactions** are always on, because insights need them. **Account identity** and **Statements** are up to you.
6. Tap **Allow and continue**.

NURU then imports your history. Your insights and charts include the account once it finishes.

## Good to know

- You can link each bank only once.
- To disconnect, tap **Disconnect** on the account. NURU stops reading it and removes its transactions from your insights. You must keep at least one account linked.
- NURU runs in a sandbox for now, so test login details work and the history is sample data.
