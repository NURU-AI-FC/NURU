# Reading your insights and the 3 month comparison

**Insights** shows where your money comes from and where it goes, across your personal accounts. Business money is shown on the Business page.

## On the web app

1. Open **Insights** from the menu.
2. Pick a period at the top: **Daily**, **Weekly**, **Monthly** or **Yearly**.
3. Read the four numbers at the top:
   - **Money in** and **Money out** for the period.
   - **Net**, what came in minus what went out.
   - **Top category**, where you spent the most.
4. Tap a category under **By category** to show only its transactions in the list below.

You can also open **Bills**, **Where you spend**, **Health score**, **Budgets**, **Goals** and **Monthly report** from the bar under the title.

## The 3 month comparison

The **Spending, last 3 months** chart puts this month next to the two months before it.

- Each month is split into weeks: **W1** is days 1 to 7, **W2** days 8 to 14, **W3** days 15 to 21, and **W4** the rest of the month.
- Under the chart you see each month's total spending.
- This month only counts what you have spent so far.

## Other charts

- **Next 30 days** shows where your balance is heading, with your lowest point.
- **When you spend** shows the last 4 weeks by day. Darker means more.

## On the phone app

Open the **Insights** tab to see what you spent, how it compares with last time, your categories, budgets, goals and health score. For the 3 month comparison, ask NURU "Compare my last 3 months".
