# Resetting your face check or PIN

This article shows you how to update or reset your transaction PIN and face check in Settings.

Your transaction PIN and face check protect every transfer sent from NURU. You can update your 4-digit PIN or re-scan your face check whenever you need to.

## How to change your transaction PIN

1. Open **Settings** from the main menu.
2. Tap **Security centre**.
3. Tap **Transaction PIN**.
4. Enter your current 4-digit PIN.
5. Enter your new 4-digit PIN. Avoid simple combinations like 1234 or repeated numbers.

## How to update your face check

1. Open **Settings** and tap **Security centre**.
2. Tap **Face check for transfers**.
3. Position your face in the centre of the circle on camera.
4. Hold still while NURU captures 3 angles to update your face template.

## Freezing transfers in an emergency

If you lose your phone or suspect unauthorized activity, you can pause transfers immediately:

1. Go to Settings > **Security centre**.
2. Tap **Freeze my account**.
3. Tap **Freeze now** to stop all transfers for 24 hours and log out other devices.
