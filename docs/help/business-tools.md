# Business tools

If you run a business, NURU shows your business money next to your personal money. Business spending is kept out of your personal insights.

## Set up your business

1. On the web app, open **Business** from the menu.
2. Tap **Set up your business**.
3. Enter your **Business name**. The **RC number** is optional.
4. Pick what kind of business it is.
5. Add your **Staff**, **Monthly sales** and **Who are your customers?**
6. Tap **Continue**.

You can change these later under **Business details**.

## What you see

The Business page shows your **Profit** for the last full month, your **Business cash** and how many days of runway it gives you, your **Unpaid invoices**, and your **Payroll due**.

## The tools

- **Payroll**: check this month's pay for each staff member, then approve it with your transaction PIN. Each person gets a payslip.
- **Vendors**: track bills you owe suppliers.
- **Invoices**: record money customers owe you.
- **Expenses**: see payments from your business account.
- **12-month plan**: a cash forecast and what is coming up.
- **Decision Room**: compare two options with your own numbers and get NURU's verdict.
- **Investor prep**: a one-page summary and questions to expect.

You need a transaction PIN to run payroll. Create one in **Settings**, then **Security**.

## On the phone app

The **Business** screen shows your profit, cash, unpaid invoices, payroll and advertising. The tools are on the web app for now. Tap **Ask NURU about my business** for a quick summary.
