# Business tools: salaries, staff, ads and reminders

This article explains how to manage payroll, staff payslips, advertising spend, and business reminders in NURU.

If you run a business, NURU provides dedicated tools on the **Business** page to manage staff salaries, track marketing expenses, and schedule meetings.

## How to run payroll and pay staff salaries

1. Tap **Business** in the main navigation menu.
2. Tap **Payroll** (or **Run payroll**).
3. Review total net salaries, PAYE tax, employee pension (8%), employer pension (10%), and NHF (2.5%).
4. Review your staff list and tap any staff member to view their individual payslip.
5. Tap **Approve** and enter your 4-digit transaction PIN to complete payment.
6. Once approved, staff receive SMS payslips automatically.

## Tracking advertising spend

1. Go to the **Business** dashboard.
2. Scroll to the **Advertising** section.
3. View total 90-day ad spend and channel breakdown (such as Instagram, Facebook, or Google ads).
4. Tap **Is my advertising paying off?** to analyze return on investment in Ask NURU.

## Managing business reminders and meetings

1. Check the **Investor meetings** section on the Business dashboard to view upcoming scheduled meetings.
2. Tap **Planner** to add new meetings or set payment reminders.
3. You can also ask NURU in chat to set reminders for bill due dates or supplier payments.
