# Reading your insights

This article explains how to view your financial insights, spending categories, and 3-month comparison.

The Insights screen analyses your income and expenses across all linked accounts so you can track where your money goes.

## How to check your insights

1. Tap **Insights** in the navigation menu.
2. Choose your preferred timeframe at the top: **Daily**, **Weekly**, **Monthly**, or **Yearly**.
3. Review your key numbers: **Money in**, **Money out**, **Net balance**, and **Top category**.
4. Scroll to **By category** to see bar charts of your spending by category.
5. Tap any category name to view all transactions in that category.

## Reading the 3-month comparison and forecast

- **3-month comparison**: Look at the "Spending over 3 months" chart to see your weekly breakdown (W1 to W4) across the current month and previous two months.
- **Forecast**: View the 30-day cash flow projection chart to see your estimated lowest balance and end-of-month balance.
- **Heat map**: Check the 4-week heat map grid to see which days of the week you spend the most money.
