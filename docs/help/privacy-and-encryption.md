# Privacy: what NURU stores and how chats are encrypted

This article explains what data NURU stores, how your chats are encrypted, and how to control your privacy.

NURU follows the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023 to keep your financial information private and secure.

## What NURU stores and protects

- **Chat encryption**: Your chats with NURU are encrypted so only you can read them. Zenith staff cannot view your chat messages.
- **Masked account numbers**: Account numbers are masked before being processed by the assistant.
- **No data selling**: NURU never sells or shares your personal data with advertisers or third parties.

## How to adjust your privacy settings

1. Open **Settings** and tap **Privacy**.
2. Toggle **Save chat history** on or off. Turning it off deletes saved chat history.
3. Toggle **Use my data for insights** to control personal financial analytics.
4. Toggle **Help improve NURU** to allow or disallow anonymous feedback data (which never includes your name or BVN).

## Downloading or deleting your data

1. Go to Settings > **Privacy**.
2. Tap **Download my data** to download a complete JSON file containing your transactions, chats, and settings.
3. Tap **Delete chat history** to permanently remove all saved chat conversations.
