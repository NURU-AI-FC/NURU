# Sending money from Ask NURU

This article explains how to send money in Ask NURU, how PIN and face check protect you, and what happens after wrong tries.

You can ask NURU to prepare a payment directly in chat. NURU drafts the transfer for you, but no money ever leaves your account until you confirm the payment, pass a face check, and enter your transaction PIN.

## How to send money in chat

1. In Ask NURU, type who you want to pay and the amount (or select an offered payment suggestion).
2. Choose a saved payee or enter the recipient's bank, account number, and name.
3. Enter what the payment is for, as every transfer requires a description.
4. Tap **Confirm and send** (or **Review and send**).
5. Complete the face check by looking at your device camera.
6. Enter your 4-digit transaction PIN to complete the transfer.

If this is your first transfer, NURU will guide you through setting up a 4-digit PIN and enrolling your face template first.

## Security and lockout rules

- Every transfer requires both a face check and your transaction PIN.
- If you enter the wrong PIN or face check fails 3 times in a row, transfers are paused for 30 minutes to protect your money.
- If your account is locked out or paused, wait 30 minutes for the pause to expire or call Zenith support at +234 1 278 7000.
