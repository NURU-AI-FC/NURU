# Talking to NURU by voice

You can speak to NURU instead of typing, and NURU can read its answers aloud.

## Speak in Ask NURU

1. Open **Ask NURU** and tap the microphone.
2. Ask your question. NURU sends it by itself when you stop talking.

## Hear answers read aloud

- Tap the speaker under any answer to hear it. Tap it again to stop.
- To hear every answer, tap the speaker at the top of Ask NURU on the phone. On the web, tap the language button and turn on **Read answers aloud**.

## Open the voice assistant

The voice assistant greets you, listens, answers by voice, then listens again for a follow-up. It opens when you:

- double tap an empty part of the screen, or double click it on the web
- press **Alt+N** on the web
- say "Hey NURU" (see below)

## "Hey NURU"

- **Phone:** works while NURU is open on screen, not when it is closed or locked. Turn it on or off in **Settings**, then **Language and voice**, then **Listen for "Hey NURU"**. Allow the microphone and speech recognition.
- **Web:** off by default. Open the voice assistant and turn on **Listen for "Hey NURU" while this tab is open**. It needs Chrome, Edge or Safari.

Your phone or browser's own speech service hears the phrase.

## Back Tap on iPhone

1. In the Shortcuts app, make a shortcut that opens the link `nuru://assistant`.
2. In iPhone **Settings**, go to **Accessibility**, **Touch**, then **Back Tap**.
3. Pick **Double Tap** or **Triple Tap** and choose your shortcut.

Now tapping the back of your iPhone opens the NURU voice assistant.
