# Linking a bank or OPay account

This article shows you how to connect your external bank or wallet account to NURU using open banking.

NURU uses Mono open banking to securely read your account balances and transactions. This read-only connection helps NURU build financial insights and spending summaries. NURU can never transfer money from a linked account without your transaction PIN and face check.

## How to link your account

1. Go to Settings and tap **Linked Accounts**.
2. Tap **Link another account** or the **+** button.
3. Choose your bank or wallet from the list (such as Zenith Bank, Access Bank, GTBank, First Bank, UBA, Kuda, OPay, or Moniepoint), or search by name.
4. Enter your internet banking user ID and password on the secure bank login screen.
5. Review the permissions requested (Balances, Transactions, Account identity, and Statements) and tap **Allow and continue**.
6. Wait a moment while NURU imports your recent transactions.

## Good to know

- Open banking access is read-only.
- NURU never sees or stores your bank login password.
- Account access expires automatically after 12 months unless you renew it.
- You can disconnect any linked account at any time from **Linked Accounts**.
