# Resetting your face check or PIN in Settings

Your transaction PIN and face check protect every transfer. You can change either one in Settings.

## Change your transaction PIN

On the web app:

1. Open **Settings**, then **Security**.
2. Tap **Transaction PIN**.
3. Enter your current PIN.
4. Choose a new 4 digit PIN. You will see **PIN saved**.

On the phone app:

1. Open the menu, then **Settings**, then **Security**.
2. Under **Transfers**, tap **Change transaction PIN**.
3. Enter your current PIN, then your new 4 digit PIN.

You need your current PIN to choose a new one. If you have forgotten it, chat with us from **Help** in Settings.

## Redo your face check

On the web app:

1. Open **Settings**, then **Security**.
2. Tap **Face check for transfers**.
3. Allow the camera and centre your face in good light.
4. Hold still until NURU has taken three captures. You will see **Face check saved**.

On the phone app, the **Face check** switch under **Transfers** sets up a face check if you do not have one yet. To redo one you already have, use the web app.

## Good to know

- NURU saves a face template, not a photo, and keeps it encrypted.
- If transfers are paused after 3 failed checks, wait 30 minutes and try again.
- If you think someone else has access, tap **Freeze my account** on the same screen. Transfers stop for 24 hours and your other devices are signed out.
